VTHAI is Thai 25 line driver for DOS, developed by Wichai Patipaporn at Computer Service Center and Faculty of Engineering, Chulalongkorn University. This software available as freeware and was shipped with CU Writer.

The driver support Hercules, EGA and VGA graphic card. Support both Kaset (เกษตร) and TIS-620 (สมอ.) character encoding.

Some files are from https://vetusware.com/download/VTHAI%20Multi%20TH/?id=18175
